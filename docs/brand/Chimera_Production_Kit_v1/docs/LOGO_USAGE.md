# Logo and Creature Usage

2026-10-24: use the refreshed exported marks and icons, preserving the fuller
central lion mane, broad feline jaw and layered metallic wings. Do not substitute
the historical reference-board/PDF artwork. The composite SVG retains the
original raster; the refinement overlay is editable, not an AI regeneration.

## Identity anatomy
- Lion: strength, execution, build.
- Eagle: vision, elevation, Air Force lineage.
- Dragon: intelligence, recombination, transformation.
- Wings: aviation context and forward motion.

## Use the full creature when
- marketing hero art
- splash/about screens
- launch material
- decks and posters
- internal program patches or morale art

## Use the compact mark when
- application chrome
- favicon/PWA icon
- small navigation surfaces
- badges and status surfaces

## Clear space
Keep at least one lion-eye-height of clear space around the compact mark. Do not place the mark inside extra shields, circles, USAF insignia or camouflage motifs.

## Do not
- change the animal trio
- remove the wings from the hero creature
- recolor the creature in arbitrary rainbow gradients
- use orange on every interactive element
- add literal aircraft silhouettes to the primary logo
- use the tagline as part of the legal wordmark
